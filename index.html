Cooperative Profile

Built by 24 founding members, run by three retired HR practitioners

Strellas was organized by a group of twenty-four (24) professionals and residents of Davao City, and registered with the Cooperative Development Authority (CDA) in November 2008, then re-registered on December 28, 2009.

Its earliest operation started on March 16, 2009 in Cagayan de Oro, on banana farm operations in Talakag, Baungon, and Libona.

Management of the Cooperative sits with three (3) HR practitioners, now retired from their respective organizations and fully engaged with the Cooperative, while other cooperators assist with management, recruitment, and marketing.

On January 28, 2020, the CDA granted a Certificate of Registration of Amendment changing the Cooperative's name from Strellas Multi-Purpose Cooperative to Strellas Labor Service Cooperative.

Cooperative Membership

Two membership categories

The Cooperative recognizes two membership categories for participation and capital build-up.

01

Regular Members

Members who satisfy the prescribed pre-membership training, fees, and share capital requirements (at least ₱12,500, equivalent to 125 shares).

02

Associate Members

Members who complete the membership education seminar, pay a one-time ₱500 fee, and join the capital build-up program.

Organizational Structure

How the Cooperative is governed

1

General Assembly

The General Assembly sits above the Audit Committee, Board of Directors, and Election Committee, and oversees the Ethics, Mediation & Conciliation, GAD, and Education & Training Committees.

2

General Manager & Assistant General Manager

Day-to-day operations are led by the General Manager and Assistant General Manager, reporting up through the Board of Directors.

3

Finance, Admin/HR & Operations

Three managers — Finance & Accounting, Admin/HR, and Operations — run the Cooperative's day-to-day departments.
