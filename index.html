About STRELLAS

A staffing partner built on two decades of trust

Strellas Labor Service Cooperative is a corporation with an authorized capital stock of twelve million pesos (₱12,000,000.00), established on September 22, 2004.

Over the years we've built our reputation on more than maintenance staffing alone — we place trained, compliant personnel across a wide range of disciplines for clients throughout Mindanao and the Visayas.
